PDEFIND and Weak SINDy documentation
==========================================

These two notebooks demonstrate the use of pysindy to discover
partial differential equations (PDEs) from data.  The first notebook
recreates the results from 
Rudy et al. (2017) "Data-driven discovery of partial differential equations".

The second adapts several examples from the former and additional ones from
Reinbold et al. (2020) "Using noisy or incomplete data to discover models of spatiotemporal dynamics".

Examples are run on python 3.12.  See requirements.txt for dependencies.
